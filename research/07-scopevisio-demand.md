# What Scopevisio's customers actually run

## The two numbers that set the roadmap

**~13** — Scopevisio's total third-party integrations, as a flat partner list
with no marketplace UX. For comparison: DATEV Marktplatz ~200+, lexoffice 150+,
weclapp and Xentral both run categorised marketplaces.

German trade press (erp-software.org, 2026) says it plainly: Scopevisio is
*"weakly equipped on this front and offers few further integrations."* Capterra
tallies eight.

**~5,000 of 7,500** — customers that are **Dienstleister** (services and
consulting). Trade is a minority; hospitality and health smaller still. Any
commerce connector therefore serves the *smallest* relevant slice.

## What Scopevisio ships, and what it does not

Ships: Finanzbuchhaltung (GoBD, DATEV export, ZUGFeRD/XRechnung), CRM, DMS/ECM
(C5 attested), Projektmanagement with time tracking, Personal/HR, Warenwirtschaft.

**Explicitly does not**, and fills by partner or acquisition:

| Gap | How it is filled |
|---|---|
| Payroll | "interfaces to external payroll" — only Eurodata, a narrow niche |
| POS | acquired act'o-soft → Scopevisio Retail (Oct 2025) |
| Hotel PMS | acquired Filosof (2018) |
| Consolidation | LucaNet partnership |
| Banking | finAPI (PSD2) |
| Corporate cards | Pliant (since June 2024) |
| Credit data | Creditreform |
| OCR / invoice capture | invoicefetcher® |
| BI | Microsoft Power BI |
| E-signature | **nothing at all** |

Existing integrations in full: Magento, Drupal, WooCommerce, eBay, Amazon,
Microsoft 365, Power BI, HubSpot, LucaNet, DATEV, Creditreform, invoicefetcher®,
finAPI, Eurodata, Pliant. **No Shopify. No Shopware.**

## The filter that kills half the obvious ideas

Do not connect what Scopevisio already sells. A Clockodo or Asana connector
competes with a module.

## Ranked by (customers served × admitted gap × competitor presence)

1. **Payroll — Personio, HRworks, DATEV LODAS.** The HR module advertises
   external payroll interfaces and has one narrow partner. Serves all ~5,000
   service firms. Widest gap between promise and reality.
2. **E-signature — DocuSign, Skribble.** Zero integrations against one of the
   most mature modules. Every vertical, small build.
3. **Travel & expense — Circula, Moss.** Pliant covers cards; the category
   leaders are absent.
4. **Shopware, then Otto and Kaufland.** Competitors carry both major shop
   systems; Scopevisio carries neither.
5. **Care documentation — MEDIFOX DAN, Connext Vivendi.** Small, sticky, and no
   clinical capability to defend. MEDIFOX DAN alone is used by 13,000+ providers.

## Strategy context

All 2026 public messaging is about becoming an "AI-first ERP"; customers are
invited to shape a 2027 roadmap. No public marketplace, app directory or
developer programme was found — which is itself the finding.

## Caveat

Part C (direct customer demand) is thin: Scopevisio has almost no public
review-site footprint, no G2 profile was found. Absence of complaints is not
evidence of satisfaction — the review base is too small to conclude either way.
